📖 Detailed Guide: How to Get the Unlock All 5 Core Craftspeople

The Unlock All 5 Core Craftspeople is a npc unlock in the progression category. Rescue all five core NPCs: Blacksmith, Alchemist, Carpenter, Farmer, and Hunter. Unlocks the full crafting ecosystem.

Preparation

Before you start working toward the Unlock All 5 Core Craftspeople, make sure you have:

  • Player Level: 5+ (higher is always better)
  • Flame Altar: Level 3 minimum
  • NPC: No specific NPC required
  • Food Buffs: Always have 3-4 food buffs active before challenging content

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Step 1: 1. Rescue Blacksmith — Ancient Vault north of Cinder Vault (-150, 80)

Step 2: 2. Rescue Alchemist — Ancient Vault west of Springlands Spire (-260, -40)

Step 3: 3. Rescue Carpenter — Ancient Vault southwest of Low Meadows Spire (-80, -120)

Step 4: 4. Rescue Farmer — Ancient Vault north of Springlands Spire (50, -180)

Step 5: 5. Rescue Hunter — Ancient Vault east of Springlands Spire (120, -40)

Step 6: Build individual sheltered rooms for each NPC (walls + roof + bed required)

Tips & Tricks

  • Place temporary Flame Altars near resource-rich areas or boss arenas for easy respawn.
  • Cook food buffs before resource farming — the right buffs can double your yield per trip.

What's Next After Getting the Unlock All 5 Core Craftspeople?

Once you've obtained the Unlock All 5 Core Craftspeople, your next goals should be:

  • Use your new unlock to access previously gated content
  • Continue advancing through the biome progression: Springlands → Revelwoods → Nomad Highlands → Kindlewastes
