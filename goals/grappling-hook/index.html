📖 Detailed Guide: How to Get the Craft Grappling Hook

The Craft Grappling Hook is a crafting in the progression category. Essential mobility tool. Allows reaching elevated areas and crossing gaps. Unlocked after Blacksmith rescue.

Preparation

Before you start working toward the Craft Grappling Hook, make sure you have:

  • Player Level: 1+ (higher is always better)
  • Flame Altar: Level 1 minimum
  • NPC: Blacksmith must be rescued and placed at your base
  • Materials: Gather all 3 material types listed above before starting
  • Food Buffs: Always have 3-4 food buffs active before challenging content

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Step 1: Rescue Blacksmith

Step 2: Collect Metal Scraps from broken structures and ruins

Step 3: Craft String from plant fibers

Step 4: Craft at Blacksmith workstation

Tips & Tricks

  • Place temporary Flame Altars near resource-rich areas or boss arenas for easy respawn.
  • Cook food buffs before resource farming — the right buffs can double your yield per trip.

What's Next After Getting the Craft Grappling Hook?

Once you've obtained the Craft Grappling Hook, your next goals should be:

  • Use your new unlock to access previously gated content
  • Continue advancing through the biome progression: Springlands → Revelwoods → Nomad Highlands → Kindlewastes
