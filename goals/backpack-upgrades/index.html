📖 Detailed Guide: How to Get the Unlock All Backpack Upgrades

The Unlock All Backpack Upgrades is a upgrade in the progression category. Expand your inventory from the base size to Large Backpack (+24 slots). Requires Hunter and Bard rescues.

Preparation

Before you start working toward the Unlock All Backpack Upgrades, make sure you have:

  • Player Level: 12+ (higher is always better)
  • Flame Altar: Level 4 minimum
  • NPC: Hunter must be rescued and placed at your base
  • Materials: Gather all 2 material types listed above before starting
  • Food Buffs: Always have 3-4 food buffs active before challenging content

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Step 1: Rescue Hunter (Springlands, rescue order #5) for Small Backpack

Step 2: Craft Small Backpack at Hunter (Tanning Station)

Step 3: Upgrade to Medium Backpack with more Leather + Linen

Step 4: Rescue Bard (Blackmire — requires Level 12+ and Revelwood Spire)

Step 5: Craft Large Backpack (+24 slots) at Bard

Tips & Tricks

  • Place temporary Flame Altars near resource-rich areas or boss arenas for easy respawn.
  • Cook food buffs before resource farming — the right buffs can double your yield per trip.

What's Next After Getting the Unlock All Backpack Upgrades?

Once you've obtained the Unlock All Backpack Upgrades, your next goals should be:

  • Use your new unlock to access previously gated content
  • Continue advancing through the biome progression: Springlands → Revelwoods → Nomad Highlands → Kindlewastes
