📖 Detailed Guide: How to Get the Unlock Blacksmith

The Unlock Blacksmith is a npc unlock in the progression category. The Blacksmith is the most important NPC — unlocks Glider, Grappling Hook, metal tools, and all weapon/armor crafting.

Preparation

Before you start working toward the Unlock Blacksmith, make sure you have:

  • Player Level: 1+ (higher is always better)
  • Flame Altar: Level 1 minimum
  • NPC: No specific NPC required
  • Materials: Gather all 1 material types listed above before starting
  • Food Buffs: Always have 3-4 food buffs active before challenging content

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Step 1: Exit Cinder Vault and commune with The Flame

Step 2: Place your first Flame Altar

Step 3: Craft a Summoning Staff (1 Twig — auto-recipe)

Step 4: Travel north from the starting area to the Ancient Vault

Step 5: Navigate the vault and rescue Oswald Anders

Step 6: Return to base and use Summoning Staff to place the Blacksmith

Tips & Tricks

  • Place temporary Flame Altars near resource-rich areas or boss arenas for easy respawn.
  • Cook food buffs before resource farming — the right buffs can double your yield per trip.

What's Next After Getting the Unlock Blacksmith?

Once you've obtained the Unlock Blacksmith, your next goals should be:

  • Use your new unlock to access previously gated content
  • Continue advancing through the biome progression: Springlands → Revelwoods → Nomad Highlands → Kindlewastes
