📖 Detailed Guide: How to Get the Upgrade Flame Altar to Level 3

The Upgrade Flame Altar to Level 3 is a upgrade in the progression category. Flame Altar Level 3 unlocks Revelwoods access, Iron crafting tier, and mid-game content. Critical progression milestone.

Preparation

Before you start working toward the Upgrade Flame Altar to Level 3, make sure you have:

  • Player Level: 5+ (higher is always better)
  • Flame Altar: Level 2 minimum
  • NPC: No specific NPC required
  • Materials: Gather all 4 material types listed above before starting
  • Food Buffs: Always have 3-4 food buffs active before challenging content

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Step 1: Upgrade to Flame Altar Level 2 first (5 Shroud Liquid, 10 Stone, 5 Wood)

Step 2: Farm Shroud Liquid from Shroud enemies

Step 3: Farm Shroud Spores from Shroud enemies and plants

Step 4: Gather Stone and Wood from anywhere

Step 5: Interact with Flame Altar and select Upgrade

Tips & Tricks

  • Place temporary Flame Altars near resource-rich areas or boss arenas for easy respawn.
  • Cook food buffs before resource farming — the right buffs can double your yield per trip.

What's Next After Getting the Upgrade Flame Altar to Level 3?

Once you've obtained the Upgrade Flame Altar to Level 3, your next goals should be:

  • Use your new unlock to access previously gated content
  • Continue advancing through the biome progression: Springlands → Revelwoods → Nomad Highlands → Kindlewastes
