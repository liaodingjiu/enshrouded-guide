📖 Detailed Guide: How to Get the Radiant Paladin Armor Set

The Radiant Paladin Armor Set is a armor set in the equipment category. Endgame heavy armor set that scales from level 13 to 25. One of only three scaling armor sets in the game. Found in gold chests throughout Albaneve Summits.

Preparation

Before you start working toward the Radiant Paladin Armor Set, make sure you have:

  • Player Level: 25+ (higher is always better)
  • Flame Altar: Level 5 minimum
  • NPC: Blacksmith must be rescued and placed at your base
  • Materials: Gather all 3 material types listed above before starting
  • Food Buffs: Always have 3-4 food buffs active before challenging content

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Step 1: Reach Level 13+ (scaling continues to 25)

Step 2: Unlock Blacksmith (Springlands — rescue order #1)

Step 3: Advance through Springlands → Revelwoods → Nomad Highlands → Kindlewastes → Albaneve Summits

Step 4: Upgrade Flame Altar to Level 5

Step 5: Farm gold chests in Albaneve Summits for Radiant Paladin pieces

Step 6: Craft any missing pieces at Blacksmith if blueprint drops

Tips & Tricks

  • Place temporary Flame Altars near resource-rich areas or boss arenas for easy respawn.
  • Cook food buffs before resource farming — the right buffs can double your yield per trip.
  • Scaling armor sets (like Radiant Paladin and Eagle Eye) are worth farming early — they scale with you from Level 13 to 25.

What's Next After Getting the Radiant Paladin Armor Set?

Once you've obtained the Radiant Paladin Armor Set, your next goals should be:

  • Upgrade your remaining gear slots to match
  • Test your new equipment against the next boss in progression
  • Optimize gems and food buffs for your upgraded build
