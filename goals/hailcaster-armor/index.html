📖 Detailed Guide: How to Get the Hailcaster Armor Set

The Hailcaster Armor Set is a armor set in the equipment category. Fixed level 23 light armor. Best mage DPS armor before endgame. Found in Kindlewastes gold chests.

Preparation

Before you start working toward the Hailcaster Armor Set, make sure you have:

  • Player Level: 23+ (higher is always better)
  • Flame Altar: Level 4 minimum
  • NPC: Alchemist must be rescued and placed at your base
  • Materials: Gather all 2 material types listed above before starting
  • Food Buffs: Always have 3-4 food buffs active before challenging content

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Step 1: Advance through Revelwoods → Nomad Highlands → Kindlewastes

Step 2: Upgrade Flame Altar to Level 4

Step 3: Farm gold chests in Kindlewastes for Hailcaster pieces

Tips & Tricks

  • Place temporary Flame Altars near resource-rich areas or boss arenas for easy respawn.
  • Cook food buffs before resource farming — the right buffs can double your yield per trip.
  • Scaling armor sets (like Radiant Paladin and Eagle Eye) are worth farming early — they scale with you from Level 13 to 25.

What's Next After Getting the Hailcaster Armor Set?

Once you've obtained the Hailcaster Armor Set, your next goals should be:

  • Upgrade your remaining gear slots to match
  • Test your new equipment against the next boss in progression
  • Optimize gems and food buffs for your upgraded build
