📖 Detailed Guide: How to Get the Build a Castle Base

The Build a Castle Base is a base in the building category. Build a large fortified castle base. Choose a strategic location with natural defenses and resource access.

Preparation

Before you start working toward the Build a Castle Base, make sure you have:

  • Player Level: 10+ (higher is always better)
  • Flame Altar: Level 3 minimum
  • NPC: Carpenter must be rescued and placed at your base
  • Materials: Gather all 3 material types listed above before starting
  • Food Buffs: Always have 3-4 food buffs active before challenging content

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Step 1: Rescue Carpenter (Springlands, rescue order #3)

Step 2: Upgrade Flame Altar to Level 3 for expanded build area

Step 3: Scout a defensible location (Highland Mesa recommended)

Step 4: Place a new Flame Altar at the chosen location

Step 5: Gather Stone, Wood, and Iron Bars in bulk

Step 6: Build foundation, walls, and roof using advanced pieces from Carpenter

Step 7: Add NPC rooms, crafting stations, and storage (Magic Chests from Carpenter)

Tips & Tricks

  • Place temporary Flame Altars near resource-rich areas or boss arenas for easy respawn.
  • Cook food buffs before resource farming — the right buffs can double your yield per trip.

What's Next After Getting the Build a Castle Base?

Once you've obtained the Build a Castle Base, your next goals should be:

  • Rescue remaining NPCs and build them dedicated rooms
  • Upgrade your Flame Altar for expanded build area
  • Add crafting stations, storage, and defensive structures
