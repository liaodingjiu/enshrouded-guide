📖 Detailed Guide: How to Get the Defeat Fell Dragon

The Defeat Fell Dragon is a boss in the progression category. Endgame multi-phase boss. Kindlewastes. Requires max preparation. Drops legendary materials.

Preparation

Before you start working toward the Defeat Fell Dragon, make sure you have:

  • Player Level: 30+ (higher is always better)
  • Flame Altar: Level 5 minimum
  • NPC: No specific NPC required
  • Materials: Gather all 2 material types listed above before starting
  • Food Buffs: Always have 3-4 food buffs active before challenging content

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Step 1: Reach Level 30

Step 2: Max out Flame Altar level

Step 3: Equip full fire resist gear

Step 4: Craft 10+ Greater Healing Potions (Alchemist)

Step 5: Cook max food buffs

Step 6: Place temp Flame Altar near Dragon's Peak

Step 7: Phase 1: Dodge fire breath, attack during recovery

Step 8: Phase 2: Move constantly during flight phase, ranged attacks

Step 9: Phase 3 (Enrage): Save burst damage, avoid lava pools

Tips & Tricks

  • Place temporary Flame Altars near resource-rich areas or boss arenas for easy respawn.
  • Cook food buffs before resource farming — the right buffs can double your yield per trip.
  • Bring 50+ arrows even for melee builds — some bosses have flying phases that require ranged attacks.

What's Next After Getting the Defeat Fell Dragon?

Once you've obtained the Defeat Fell Dragon, your next goals should be:

  • Use your new unlock to access previously gated content
