📖 Detailed Guide: How to Get the Wailing Blade (Legendary)

The Wailing Blade (Legendary) is a weapon in the equipment category. S-Tier Two-Handed Greatsword. Highest raw damage in the game, cleaves 3 targets per swing. Legendary version drops from Fell Dragon.

Preparation

Before you start working toward the Wailing Blade (Legendary), make sure you have:

  • Player Level: 30+ (higher is always better)
  • Flame Altar: Level 5 minimum
  • NPC: Blacksmith must be rescued and placed at your base
  • Materials: Gather all 2 material types listed above before starting
  • Food Buffs: Always have 3-4 food buffs active before challenging content

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Step 1: Reach Level 25-30

Step 2: Upgrade Flame Altar to max level

Step 3: Travel to Kindlewastes — Dragon's Peak (-1400, 1600)

Step 4: Defeat Fell Dragon (~15% drop rate for legendary version)

Step 5: Alternatively craft Steel version at Blacksmith with Steel Bars + Hardened Leather

Step 6: Upgrade to Legendary with Dragon Scale x3 + Steel x20

Tips & Tricks

  • Place temporary Flame Altars near resource-rich areas or boss arenas for easy respawn.
  • Cook food buffs before resource farming — the right buffs can double your yield per trip.
  • Check the Best Weapons Tier List to see how this weapon compares to alternatives.

What's Next After Getting the Wailing Blade (Legendary)?

Once you've obtained the Wailing Blade (Legendary), your next goals should be:

  • Upgrade your remaining gear slots to match
  • Test your new equipment against the next boss in progression
  • Optimize gems and food buffs for your upgraded build
