📖 Detailed Guide: How to Get the Dragonbone Bow (Legendary)

The Dragonbone Bow (Legendary) is a weapon in the equipment category. Legendary bow dropped by Fell Dragon. Highest ranged DPS potential in the game. ~20% drop rate.

Preparation

Before you start working toward the Dragonbone Bow (Legendary), make sure you have:

  • Player Level: 30+ (higher is always better)
  • Flame Altar: Level 5 minimum
  • NPC: No specific NPC required
  • Food Buffs: Always have 3-4 food buffs active before challenging content

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Step 1: Reach Level 30

Step 2: Upgrade Flame Altar to max level

Step 3: Travel to Kindlewastes — Dragon's Peak (-1400, 1600)

Step 4: Defeat Fell Dragon (~20% drop rate)

Step 5: Repeat if not dropped (boss respawns after a few in-game days)

Tips & Tricks

  • Place temporary Flame Altars near resource-rich areas or boss arenas for easy respawn.
