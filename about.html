About Enshrouded Guides

Independent, player-tested Enshrouded resources. We build guides for players who want data, not fluff.

Who We Are

Enshrouded Guides is built by experienced Enshrouded players with 200+ hours of gameplay each. Every skill rating, boss strategy, weapon ranking, and base location on this site has been tested in-game — not scraped from a wiki or generated by AI.

The Enshrouded Guides team has 200+ hours in Enshrouded across all biomes, skill trees, and boss encounters. Every guide is tested in-game, not theorycrafted or copied from wikis.

Why This Site Exists

There are plenty of Enshrouded resources out there, but most are either outdated wikis, low-effort listicles, or AI-generated fluff. We built this site to be the opposite: accurate, data-rich, and maintained by people who actually play the game.

Our Content Principles

  • Player-tested, not copy-pasted. Every guide is verified against live game mechanics. If we haven't tested it, we don't publish it.
  • Data over opinion. We show you the numbers — damage calculations, drop rates, skill comparisons — so you can make your own decisions.
  • Updated within 48 hours of patches. Game meta changes, and so do our guides. Every page shows its last-updated date.
  • No paywalls, no forced sign-ups. All guides are free, always. We may run ads to cover hosting costs, but the content stays free.

What We Cover

Our current guide coverage includes:

  • Best Skills Tier List — Every skill ranked S to D with real mechanics explained
  • Best Weapons Guide — All weapon types ranked, crafting requirements, legendary locations
  • Base Building Guide — Best locations, NPC housing, crafting layouts, defense
  • Boss Guide — Every boss from Fell Warden to Hollow King with attack patterns and strategies
  • Beginner Walkthrough — First 10 hours step-by-step

More guides are on the way — including Fix & Performance FAQ, NPC unlock guides, resource location maps, and class build guides.
