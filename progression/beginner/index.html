📖 Enshrouded Beginner Progression Path — What to Do First

Starting Enshrouded can be overwhelming. This progression path guides you from exiting Cinder Vault through establishing your first base, rescuing the Blacksmith, crafting the Glider and Grappling Hook, and preparing for the Fell Warden — all in the optimal order.

Your Optimal Action Plan

Based on the new player progression path, here's what you should focus on — in priority order:

  1. Complete the tutorial — Exit Cinder Vault, commune with The Flame, place your first Flame Altar.
  2. Rescue the Blacksmith — This is your #1 priority NPC. Unlocks Glider, Grappling Hook, and all weapon crafting.
  3. Craft the Glider and Grappling Hook — These two items transform exploration. Get them before doing anything else.
  4. Unlock Double Jump and Water Aura — The two most impactful early skill points in the game.
  5. Upgrade Flame Altar to Level 2 — Extends Shroud timer and unlocks basic crafting upgrades.

Key Milestones on This Path

As you follow this progression path, you'll hit several major milestones:

  • First Flame Altar: Your permanent base location. Choose carefully — near wood, stone, and water.
  • Blacksmith Rescued: Game-changer. Glider + Grappling Hook open the vertical world.
  • First Boss Down: Fell Warden marks the transition from tutorial to real game.

Common Pitfalls to Avoid

  • Skipping Flame Altar upgrades — Each level gates crucial content. Upgrade before you need to.
  • Ignoring temporary Flame Altars — Place them near bosses and resource-rich areas. The 5-Stone cost is trivial.
  • Not cooking food buffs — 3-4 food buffs can double your effective power. Always eat before challenging content.
  • Rushing biomes underleveled — Enemies scale hard between biomes. If you're dying in 2 hits, you're underleveled or undergeared.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do first in Enshrouded?

Exit Cinder Vault → Place your first Flame Altar → Craft basic tools → Build a shelter → Rescue the Blacksmith → Craft the Glider. These 6 steps set the foundation for everything else.

How long does early game take?

Expect 3-5 hours to complete the Springlands content as a new player. Speedrunners can reach Revelwoods in under 2 hours. Take your time — the Glider and Grappling Hook transform exploration.
