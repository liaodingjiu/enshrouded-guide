Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 13, 2026

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Analytics Data

We use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use this site. Clarity collects anonymized usage data including page views, scroll depth, click patterns, and session recordings. Clarity does not use cookies for tracking and does not collect personal information such as your name, email, or IP address in a personally identifiable form.

1.2 Advertising Data

When we serve advertisements (via Google AdSense or similar platforms), third-party vendors may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or AboutAds.info.

1.3 Cookie Consent

When you first visit our site, a cookie consent banner appears. You can accept or decline non-essential cookies:

  • Accept: We set a consent cookie (365 days) and load analytics/advertising scripts.
  • Decline: We set a decline cookie (365 days) and do not load analytics/advertising scripts. Only essential cookies (such as your theme preference) are used.

Our consent cookie is called enshrouded-guide-consent and stores only your choice ("accepted" or "declined"). It does not contain personal data.

4. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of data we hold about you.
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your data.
  • Opt-out: Decline non-essential cookies via our consent banner, or opt out of personalized ads via Google Ads Settings.
  • Complaint: File a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us.

5. Data Retention

Analytics data (Clarity) is retained by Microsoft according to their retention policies. Cookie consent preferences are stored locally in your browser for 365 days. We do not maintain a separate database of user information.

6. Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

For privacy-related questions or to exercise your rights: Contact Us.
