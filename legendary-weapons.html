⚡ Quick Answer: The fastest legendary farm is the ornate gold chest in the northern shroud canyon (Albaneve Summits). Place a Flame Altar 10 feet away, loot (takes 7 seconds), quit to main menu, reload — repeat up to 6 times per minute. Ornate chests have a ~50/50 Epic/Legendary split. Regular gold chests drop ~33% Legendary. Silver chests aren't worth farming.

🥇 #1 Farm: Ornate Chest — Northern Shroud Canyon

The single best legendary farm in Enshrouded. Fastest, safest, highest drop rate.

📍 Location

Albaneve Summits — northern edge of the map, where the shroud canyon meets the desert. The chest is half-buried in sand on a ledge above the shroud. Unguarded.

⏱️ Run Time

~7 seconds per run. Place your Flame Altar ~10 feet from the chest (just outside build-block range). Loot → quit to menu → reload → repeat.

🎁 Drops

All legendary weapons from the Albaneve Summits loot pool. All three Level 25 armor sets (Elder, Radiant Paladin, Eagle Eye). 6 runs per minute maximum.

🕷️ Spider Cave — Below Mountains Spire

📍 Location

Go to the Mountains Spire, jump off south, follow the cliff face to ground level. Look for the spider cave entrance.

🎁 Drops

Regular gold chest inside. Also contains iron and coal nodes — good for dual-purpose farming.

💡 Tip

The tombgrave inside reportedly still works for legendary farming post-patches. Place an altar at the entrance.

🏛️ Sun Temple — Far East

📍 Location

Far east edge of the map. Enter the temple, run right, follow the path down stairs, through a door, turn left, then open the door on your right.

🎁 Drops

Gold chest inside a side room. Good for regular gold chest drops (~33% Legendary rate).

⚠️ Warning

Some enemies inside. Clear them once, place an altar nearby, then the run is safe.

💀 Elixir Well — Southeast Kindlewastes

📍 Location

Southeast of Kindlewastes Ancient Spire. Set a Flame Altar near the entrance.

⏱️ Run Time

~30 seconds per run. Longer than the canyon chest but drops a wider variety. Minimum weapon rarity is Epic.

🎁 Notable Drops

Nova (sword), Ice Bane, Shadowbane, Fearsome Club, Twin Soul Mace, and all Level 25 armor sets.

🗺️ More Farm Locations

🪨 Ritual Tempest Wand Spot

Southwest of Scatterbone, cliff edge where blue shroud meets red shroud. Regular gold chest on rubble. Confirmed Ritual Tempest Wand (legendary) drops by multiple players.

🏠 Brittlebush — Two Roof Chests

Southwest corner of Brittlebush. Two gold chests close together atop roofs. Good for armor and weapon dual-drops.

👁️ Quiet Caves (Cyclops Cave)

Southeast of the northern gem forge. Cyclops guards a gold chest. You can likely loot it without fighting the Cyclops if you're quick.

🔄 Relog Method — How It Works

1. Place Altar

Put a Flame Altar as close as possible to the gold chest. Stay just outside the build-block range so the chest still spawns.

2. Loot

Open the chest. Grab everything — even items you don't want can be salvaged for Runes.

3. Relog

Quit to main menu → reload your save. The chest refills. Repeat as many times as you want. No daily limit.

Pro Tip: Put on a podcast. Legendary farming is a numbers game — the more runs you do, the more legendaries you get. With the 7-second canyon chest, expect roughly 1 legendary every 2 runs (~15 seconds per legendary).

📌 Key Takeaways

🟢 Ornate > Gold > Silver

Ornate gold chests are twice as good as regular gold for legendaries. Silver chests aren't worth your time. Always prioritize ornate chests.

🟢 Relog Method Still Works

Quit to menu → reload. Chest refills. No daily limit. The 7-second canyon run yields ~1 legendary every 15 seconds on average.

🟢 Don't Over-Farm

Future updates will raise the level cap, making current legendaries obsolete. Farm what you need, then go play the game.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the single best chest to farm?
The ornate gold chest in the northern shroud canyon of Albaneve Summits. It's half-buried in sand, unguarded, takes 7 seconds per run, and drops all legendary weapons plus Level 25 armor sets.
Does quitting and reloading still reset chests?
Yes. Place a Flame Altar near the chest, loot, quit to main menu, reload. The chest refills. This works in Update 8.
What's the difference between Legendary and Epic weapons?
Legendary weapons have one additional enchantment effect compared to their Epic versions. Base damage is identical between Legendary and Epic of the same weapon type.
Are legendary weapons locked to specific chests?
No. Loot is zone-based RNG. Any gold chest in the Albaneve Summits can drop any legendary from that zone's loot pool. A few items (like Commander's Ring) are 100% fixed drops, but weapons are random.
