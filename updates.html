📦 Update 7 — What We Know

The most recent major update before 1.0. Here's what changed and what players are saying.

Update 7 is the latest major content patch for Enshrouded Early Access. While Keen Games hasn't published exhaustive official notes, the community has documented the key changes:

  • Water System Expansion — Water Wells are now buildable, Shroud-infused water nodes added to deep Shroud zones, and water is now required for several new alchemy recipes. This is the biggest mechanical addition in Update 7. See our Water Guide for full details.
  • New Crafting Recipes — Advanced potions, irrigation systems for crop farming, and water-based decorative items (fountains, pools).
  • Balance Changes — Several skill trees received adjustments. Survivor stamina skills were slightly nerfed; Mage mana regeneration was buffed. Check the Build Guides for updated skill allocations.
  • Bug Fixes — NPC pathing improvements, Shroud timer display fixed, multiplayer desync issues reduced.
  • Performance — Shader compilation stutter reduced on NVIDIA GPUs. Water rendering optimized.
📢 Official source: Follow Keen Games on Twitter and the r/Enshrouded subreddit for official patch notes and announcements.

Steam Deck Compatibility

Enshrouded runs on the Steam Deck at 30-40 FPS on low settings. The game is currently rated "Playable" (not "Verified") due to small UI text and occasional performance dips in dense areas. Community-tested optimal Steam Deck settings: 800p resolution, FSR 2.0 on Balanced, shadows Low, volumetric fog Off. With these settings, you can maintain a mostly stable 30 FPS even in the Revelwoods.
