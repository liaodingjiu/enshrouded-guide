❓ Quick Answers

Common Enshrouded questions — straight answers.

What's the best class in Enshrouded?
There's no single "best class" — Enshrouded uses a flexible skill tree system. The most versatile early-game path is Survivor (Double Jump + Dessert Stomach). For combat, Assassin (Updraft + Airborne) gives the best mobility. See full tier list →
Where should I build my first base?
Build near the starting Cinder Vault on flat ground with access to wood and stone. Avoid the Shroud — your flame altar needs to be outside Shroud zones. As you progress, build a second base near the Revelwoods for mid-game resources. Full base guide →
How do I survive the Shroud?
The Shroud timer limits how long you can stay inside. Upgrade your Flame Altar to increase the timer and access higher-level Shroud zones. Carry Shroud Survival potions for emergencies, and always know your nearest exit point before entering deep Shroud.
Can I respec my skills?
Yes. You can respec at the Flame Altar using Runes — a currency earned from defeating enemies and destroying Shroud Roots. The cost increases with each respec, so plan your build in advance. Our skill guide helps with planning →
