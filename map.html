🧑 NPC Rescue Locations

Every NPC you can rescue in Enshrouded. Rescue them in this order for the most efficient early-game progression.

NPCBiomeCoordinatesDirectionsUnlocks
Blacksmith (Rescue 1st) Springlands (-150, 80) Found in a ruined forge directly east of the Cinder Vault. The building has a distinctive anvil outside. Easy rescue — no combat required. Weapons, Armor, Tools, Grappling Hook
Hunter (Rescue 2nd) Springlands (-90, 220) Located in a ruined tower in the northern Springlands. The tower is visible from a distance. Clear the Scavenger camp at the base before climbing. Ranged Weapons, Arrows, Hunter Armor
Alchemist (Rescue 3rd) Springlands (-200, 310) Hidden in a cave system in the western Springlands. Follow the glowing mushrooms. The cave has Shroud patches — bring a Shroud Survival Potion. Potions, Magic Items, Shroud Potions
Carpenter Springlands (60, 150) Held in a Scavenger camp in the eastern Springlands. The camp has 4-5 enemies. Clear them before approaching the cage. Advanced Building, Furniture, Storage
Farmer Revelwoods (520, -180) Found in a ruined farmhouse in the central Revelwoods. The area is patrolled by wolves — clear them before the rescue. Crop Farming, Irrigation, Food Production
Collector Revelwoods (750, -350) Located in a hidden underground vault beneath a ruined temple. The entrance is behind a breakable wall — look for cracked stone blocks. Rare Materials Exchange, Collectible Tracking

💧 Water Sources

Major water sources by biome. Natural water is infinite and free — great for early game before you build a Water Well.

Water SourceBiomeCoordinatesTypeNotes
Cinder River Springlands (-50, 60) River Closest water source to spawn. Runs north-south through the central Springlands. Safe area — no enemies near the water.
Spring Lakes Springlands (-180, 280) Lake Three connected small lakes. Best early-game water farming spot — collect from all three in a 2-minute loop.
Revelwood Lake Revelwoods (560, -230) Lake Largest lake in the Revelwoods. Watch for wolf patrols on the shore. Great fishing spot — uncommon and rare fish spawn here.
Highland Springs Nomad Highlands (900, -650) Spring Small but reliable spring. Surrounded by iron ore deposits — combine water collection with mining runs.
Kindlewastes Oasis Kindlewastes (-1200, 1400) Oasis Rare water in the desert biome. Highly contested in multiplayer. Legendary fish can be caught here. Build a base nearby.
Shroud Spring Shroud Depths (150, -1950) Shroud Water Produces Shroud-infused water for advanced alchemy. Collection drains Shroud timer 2× faster — be prepared with potions.

📖 How to Navigate Enshrouded

Enshrouded doesn't have a built-in minimap with coordinates displayed by default. Here's how to use this page effectively:

  1. Open your in-game map (default key: M). Your current coordinates are displayed in the corner of the map screen.
  2. Click the 📋 Copy button next to any location on this page to copy its coordinates.
  3. Navigate toward the coordinates — the first number is East/West (X axis), the second is North/South (Y axis).
  4. Place a Flame Altar at key locations to create fast-travel points. You can fast-travel between any of your active Flame Altars from the map screen.

For a visual interactive map with zoom and pan, we recommend MapGenie's Enshrouded Map (external, free). Our page above provides all the coordinates you need for copy-paste navigation, which is often faster than referencing a visual map.
