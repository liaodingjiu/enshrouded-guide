Enshrouded NPC Unlock Guide — All Craftspeople & Rescue Order

Every NPC in Enshrouded: where to find them, what order to rescue them, and what they unlock. 5 craftspeople + 1 Collector + 1 Bard + 5 assistants + 10 villagers — all with exact vault locations.

⚡ Quick Answer: Rescue the Blacksmith first — he unlocks the Glider and Grappling Hook, which you need to reach every other NPC vault. Then: Alchemist (healing potions) → Carpenter (Magic Chests) → Farmer (sustainable food) → Hunter (backpack upgrades). Craft a Summoning Staff from 1 Twig to place NPCs at your base. Each NPC needs walls + roof + bed to offer full services.

🎯 Rescue Priority Order

This is the optimal unlock order based on what each NPC gives you and when you need it.

PriorityNPCWhy This OrderKey Unlocks
1st Blacksmith (Oswald Anders) Glider + Grappling Hook required to reach other vaults. Non-negotiable first pick. Glider, Grappling Hook, metal tools, Forge, Charcoal Kiln, basic armor
2nd Alchemist (Balthazar) Healing potions and Shroud Survival Flasks keep you alive in harder zones. Health potions, buff elixirs, Shroud Survival Flask, Alchemy Station
3rd Carpenter (Cade Hawthorn) Magic Chests auto-feed crafting stations — massive QoL upgrade. Magic Chests, advanced building blocks, Kiln, Table Saw, furniture
4th Farmer (Emily Fray) Sustainable food ends the berry-picking grind. Seedbeds for flax farming. Seedbed, Oven, Fireplace, farming, advanced cooking recipes
5th Hunter (Athalan Skree) Backpack upgrades and ranged gear. Important but not urgent early game. Backpack upgrades, Drying Rack, Tanning Station, Loom, ranged armor
Pro Tip: After the Blacksmith, the order is flexible. If you're dying a lot → Alchemist next. If your inventory is always full → Carpenter (Magic Chests). If you're starving → Farmer.

🔧 Core Craftspeople — Locations & What They Unlock

All five are found in Ancient Vaults in the Springlands region. Look for the glowing door icons on your map.

⚒️ Blacksmith — Oswald Anders

Location: Ancient Vault – Blacksmith, north of the starting Cinder Vault / southwest of Springlands Ancient Spire.

Unlocks: Glider, Grappling Hook, melee weapons, metal armor, Forge, Charcoal Kiln, Smelter, Summoning Staff recipe.

Quest: "The Blacksmith's Request" — triggers automatically after placing your first Flame Altar.

🧪 Alchemist — Balthazar

Location: Ancient Vault – Alchemist, west of Springlands Ancient Spire.

Unlocks: Healing potions, Shroud Survival Flask, buff elixirs, staff charges, Alchemy Station, Laboratory.

Quest: "A Black Cauldron for the Alchemist" — requires Nomad Highlands Spire unlocked + Level 4 Flame.

🪚 Carpenter — Cade Hawthorn

Location: Ancient Vault – Carpenter, southwest of Low Meadows Ancient Spire.

Unlocks: Magic Chests (auto-feed crafting stations!), advanced building materials, Kiln, Table Saw, furniture, decorations.

Quest: "A Kiln for the Carpenter" — triggers after rescue.

🌾 Farmer — Emily Fray

Location: Ancient Vault – Farmer, north of Springlands Ancient Spire.

Unlocks: Seedbed, Farm Soil, Oven, Fireplace, Beehives, animal husbandry, advanced cooking recipes.

Quest: "Growing Stronger Together" — requires completing other NPC quests first.

🏹 Hunter — Athalan Skree

Location: Ancient Vault – Hunter, east of Springlands Ancient Spire.

Unlocks: Backpack upgrades, ranged armor, Drying Rack, Tanning Station, Loom, Spinning Wheel, arrows.

Quest: "A Tanning Station for the Hunter" — triggers after rescue.

💀 Collector — Alden Crowley

Unlocked after rescuing all five core craftspeople. Requires completing the Hollow Halls quest chain.

📍 Location

Springlands Hollow Halls, southwest of Springlands Ancient Spire. Requires Level 4 Flame and the five core craftspeople rescued.

🎁 Unlocks

Ectoplasm Press, Skull Summoning items, Hollow Halls survival gear. The Collector specializes in Shroud-related crafting and summoning items.

🎵 Bard — Valory

Found in the Blackmire region. Requires Level 12+ and access to Revelwood Ancient Spire.

📍 Location

Sunken Ancient City (Blackmire). Start the "Follow the Melody" quest after unlocking Revelwood Ancient Spire.

🎁 Unlocks

Musical instruments, Medium Backpack (+12 slots), Large Backpack (+24 slots), decorations. The Bard is worth rushing for the backpack upgrades alone.

🪄 Summoning Staff & NPC Placement

How to place NPCs at your base and what they need to function.

🔨 Crafting

Cost: 1 Twig. The cheapest craft in Enshrouded. Recipe unlocks automatically after rescuing your first NPC. Craft it at any Workbench.

🏠 Placement

Equip the Summoning Staff, open your inventory, select the NPC from the staff menu, and place them at your base. You can reposition NPCs anytime for free.

🛏️ Requirements

Each NPC needs walls + roof + bed to offer full crafting services. Some recipes require the "Sheltered" condition. Place workstations near the NPC for easiest access.

Pro Tip: Use a Flame Altar as a mobile checkpoint when traveling to distant vaults. Place it → fast travel home to sleep/repair → fast travel back → dismantle and reuse.

📌 Key Takeaways

🟢 Blacksmith Is Non-Negotiable

Without the Glider and Grappling Hook, you can't reach most other NPC vaults. Rescue the Blacksmith before doing anything else.

🟢 Summoning Staff Costs 1 Twig

It's the cheapest craft in the game. Don't forget to actually craft it — you can't place NPCs without it.

🟢 Rush the Bard for Backpack Space

Valory unlocks the Medium (+12) and Large (+24) Backpack upgrades. Worth prioritizing once you hit Level 12 and can access Blackmire.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What NPC should I rescue first?
The Blacksmith (Oswald Anders) — always. He unlocks the Glider and Grappling Hook, which are required to reach most other Ancient Vaults. Without him, you're walking everywhere.
How many NPCs are there total?
20 total: 5 core craftspeople + 1 Collector + 1 Bard + 5 assistants + 10 villagers. Core craftspeople unlock essential crafting. Villagers are cosmetic/completionist.
Can I move NPCs after placing them?
Yes. Use the Summoning Staff to pick up and reposition any NPC at any time, for free. You can also build them a new house and reassign them there.
Do NPCs need their own rooms?
They need walls, a roof, and a bed. They don't need individual rooms — you can house multiple NPCs in one large building. The "Sheltered" condition is what matters for unlocking recipes.
