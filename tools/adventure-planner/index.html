    ⚙️ How the Adventure Planner Works

    The Adventure Planner uses a rule-based priority engine to build your personalized action plan:

    1. Stage Detection — Your level, biome, Flame Altar level, and gear tier determine which of 4 game stages you're in (Early/Mid/Late/Endgame).
    2. Action Filtering — Actions that require NPCs you haven't rescued or gear you don't have are filtered out or flagged with prerequisites.
    3. Goal Bias — If you set a current goal (boss, gear, base building, etc.), actions matching that goal get boosted in the ranking.
    4. Priority Sorting — Actions are sorted by their natural progression priority, then adjusted by your specific situation. The top 5 most impactful next steps are shown.

    Pro tip: Re-run the planner whenever you complete a major milestone (boss kill, biome unlock, Flame Altar upgrade) — your plan updates dynamically.
